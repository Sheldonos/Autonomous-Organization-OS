# System Map

The federation contains 7 segments and 48 specialized operating-system pockets. Each pocket owns a workflow and teams, while reusing the canonical capability substrate.

## MAKE
Invent, research, validate, build, and launch.

| Pocket | Mission |
|---|---|
| VentureOS | Turn an opportunity or thesis into a validated, launched, measurable company or business line. |
| ResearchOS | Convert questions into evidence-grounded findings, scenarios, decisions, and reusable knowledge. |
| BuildOS | Translate product intent into secure, tested, deployed, observable software and digital systems. |
| InnovationOS | Continuously discover, evaluate, incubate, and scale strategic innovations. |
| LaunchOS | Coordinate a cross-functional product, service, or market launch from readiness through post-launch learning. |
| ConsultingOS | Run evidence-driven consulting engagements from discovery through implementation and outcome measurement. |
| Patent-to-ProductOS | Find valuable intellectual property and convert it into validated products, licenses, ventures, or partnerships. |
| LabOS | Operate R&D programs with hypotheses, experiments, evidence, reproducibility, and portfolio decisions. |

## SELL
Discover demand, win opportunities, transact, and grow revenue.

| Pocket | Mission |
|---|---|
| DealOS | Discover, qualify, pursue, negotiate, close, and operationalize profitable commercial opportunities. |
| RevenueOS | Operate the full revenue engine from ICP and demand through sales, onboarding, retention, and expansion. |
| BidOS | Find and win structured opportunities such as RFPs, RFIs, RFQs, grants, prizes, and procurement competitions. |
| CommerceOS | Select, source, price, merchandise, sell, fulfill, and retain customers around products and digital commerce. |
| PartnershipOS | Build and operate partner ecosystems, alliances, co-sell motions, and strategic collaborations. |
| PricingOS | Design and continuously improve pricing, packaging, discounting, and monetization using evidence and guardrails. |
| CustomerOS | Coordinate customer onboarding, service, success, adoption, retention, renewal, and expansion. |
| GrantOS | Discover, qualify, assemble, submit, manage, and report on grant opportunities. |

## RUN
Coordinate organizational execution, people, vendors, assets, and governance.

| Pocket | Mission |
|---|---|
| EnterpriseOS | Act as the federation control plane that senses organizational state, decomposes objectives, routes work, governs execution, and measures outcomes. |
| ExecutiveOS | Maintain executive situational awareness, decision queues, delegation, follow-through, and organizational memory. |
| ProcureOS | Turn internal needs into governed sourcing, competitive procurement, negotiation, purchase, and supplier performance. |
| TalentOS | Align workforce capabilities to strategy through planning, mobility, performance, development, automation, and hiring decisions. |
| PMO OS | Translate strategy into portfolios, programs, projects, dependencies, risks, decisions, and measurable outcomes. |
| VendorOS | Manage third-party vendors from onboarding and risk through performance, obligations, renewal, and exit. |
| SupplyOS | Coordinate demand, sourcing, supply planning, inventory, logistics, exceptions, and service levels. |
| PropertyOS | Operate real-estate opportunities and portfolios from underwriting through diligence, leasing, operations, and disposition. |
| FranchiseOS | Design, sell, launch, govern, and optimize a repeatable franchise operating network. |
| BoardOS | Prepare board governance, decision materials, action tracking, risk visibility, and institutional memory. |
| RecruitOS | Operate recruiting from demand and sourcing through assessment, offer, onboarding transition, and funnel learning. |

## PROTECT
Manage cyber, legal, compliance, IP, and enterprise risk.

| Pocket | Mission |
|---|---|
| SecurityOS | Continuously reduce cyber risk through asset visibility, detection, investigation, remediation, validation, and governance. |
| LegalOS | Coordinate legal operations, research, contracts, matters, obligations, and approvals with human legal authority preserved. |
| ComplianceOS | Translate obligations into controls, evidence, monitoring, remediation, attestations, and audit-ready reporting. |
| RiskOS | Maintain enterprise risk intelligence, scenarios, treatment plans, controls, ownership, and escalation. |
| PatentOS | Manage invention intake, prior-art and landscape research, prosecution support, portfolio intelligence, and IP obligations. |

## INVEST
Allocate capital, execute corporate development, and manage funds.

| Pocket | Mission |
|---|---|
| CapitalOS | Operate investment research and portfolio intelligence from thesis through diligence, IC decision, and monitoring. |
| CorpDevOS | Evaluate and execute build/buy/partner/invest strategies for corporate growth and strategic capability acquisition. |
| M&A OS | Manage merger and acquisition workflows from target discovery through diligence, transaction planning, and integration handoff. |
| FundOS | Operate fund intelligence and administration around mandate, pipeline, portfolio, reporting, and governance. |
| FundraiseOS | Plan and execute capital raising from investor thesis and targeting through diligence, commitments, and relationship management. |

## CREATE
Produce media, entertainment, brands, creator businesses, events, and agency work.

| Pocket | Mission |
|---|---|
| MediaOS | Operate a publication from signal detection and reporting through multi-format distribution, audience learning, and monetization. |
| StudioOS | Operate audiovisual and entertainment production from concept and IP through production, post, distribution, and franchise learning. |
| CreatorOS | Turn a creator, artist, expert, or audience into a coordinated content, partnership, product, community, and revenue business. |
| BrandOS | Maintain brand positioning, identity, messaging, asset governance, campaigns, and brand performance. |
| EventOS | Design and operate events from concept and economics through speakers, sponsors, attendees, production, and post-event monetization. |
| AgencyOS | Run a multi-client creative, marketing, product, or technology agency from pipeline through delivery, margin, and retention. |

## LEARN
Operate adaptive education, university, workforce, and accelerator systems.

| Pocket | Mission |
|---|---|
| EducationOS | Operate personalized education from curriculum and instruction through assessment, mastery, safety, credentialing, and progression. |
| LearningOS | Provide the shared lifelong-learning and adaptive mastery layer across school, university, workforce, and self-directed learning. |
| UniversityOS | Operate higher-education academic and learner workflows across programs, courses, advising, projects, credentials, and outcomes. |
| WorkforceOS | Continuously map employee capabilities to roles, learning, projects, credentials, and internal mobility. |
| AcceleratorOS | Operate venture or talent accelerators from cohort sourcing through curriculum, mentorship, milestones, demo, and alumni outcomes. |
