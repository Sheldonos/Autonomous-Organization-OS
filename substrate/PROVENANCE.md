## QisBob benchmark
The full `qisbob-quantum-mentor` skill supplied by the user is preserved as the canonical benchmark, with additive production-hardening sections and machine-checkable state/request/test companions.

## Public repository inventory
A public repository may be copied/adapted only after repository identity, license, exact revision, security/compatibility, attribution requirements and provenance mapping are verified. When that evidence does not exist, use clean-room capability design rather than implying a direct conversion.
