# CommerceOS

**Mission:** Select, source, price, merchandise, sell, fulfill, and retain customers around products and digital commerce.

## Operating loop
1. Market & product discovery
2. Sourcing
3. Unit economics & pricing
4. Catalog & storefront
5. Content & acquisition
6. Conversion optimization
7. Fulfillment
8. Retention & merchandising loop

## Shared capability substrate
This pocket does not duplicate the entire skill corpus. `skill_routes.json` selects up to 120 high-relevance canonical skills from the 3,212-skill registry. Run `python aos.py hydrate commerceos` to materialize only those skill packages into a working directory.

## Handoffs
supplyos, pricingos, revenueos, creatoros, brandos
