# VentureOS

**Mission:** Turn an opportunity or thesis into a validated, launched, measurable company or business line.

## Operating loop
1. Thesis & opportunity framing
2. Market and customer discovery
3. Business model & economics
4. Validation experiments
5. Product definition
6. Build coordination
7. Launch & GTM
8. Capital and operating cadence

## Shared capability substrate
This pocket does not duplicate the entire skill corpus. `skill_routes.json` selects up to 120 high-relevance canonical skills from the 3,212-skill registry. Run `python aos.py hydrate ventureos` to materialize only those skill packages into a working directory.

## Handoffs
researchos, buildos, revenueos, capitalos, legalos, launchos
