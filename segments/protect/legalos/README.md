# LegalOS

**Mission:** Coordinate legal operations, research, contracts, matters, obligations, and approvals with human legal authority preserved.

## Operating loop
1. Matter intake
2. Issue spotting
3. Research
4. Draft/markup
5. Review & negotiation
6. Approval/signature
7. Obligation tracking
8. Matter close & knowledge

## Shared capability substrate
This pocket does not duplicate the entire skill corpus. `skill_routes.json` selects up to 120 high-relevance canonical skills from the 3,212-skill registry. Run `python aos.py hydrate legalos` to materialize only those skill packages into a working directory.

## Handoffs
complianceos, riskos, dealos, procureos
