# Market Readiness

## Software release claim
v1.2.0 is packaged as a **production-ready software control-plane release**: complete factory structure, canonical skill accountability, runtime, policy, state, adaptive connector assimilation, proactivity, deployment manifests, tests and release evidence.

## What is not pre-certified
No generic ZIP can certify a future customer's identity system, data quality, secrets, MCP servers, write permissions, recovery targets or legal decision rights. Those are deployment acceptance items and intentionally fail closed.

## Redistribution boundary
The first-party federation/runtime/wrapper layer is separable from the preserved user-provided/upstream-origin substrate. The included provenance notices require a repository/source-level legal review before broad public redistribution of copied third-party material. Do not replace that review with an invented blanket license.

## Enterprise resilience differentiators
The market package now includes event-first bite-sized autonomy, explicit deployment drain/backout, durable effect/idempotency contracts, backup/restore and DR runbooks, hybrid compute-to-data deployment, an air-gapped cold-room profile, immutable OCI/storage policy, and egress-aware portability. These are part of the product architecture rather than sales-only claims; each becomes a tenant certification item at deployment time.
