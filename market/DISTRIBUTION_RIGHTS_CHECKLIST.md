# Distribution Rights Checklist

The OS Factory runtime, federation metadata, wrapper skills and release tooling in v1.2.0 are generated for this product. The pinned canonical substrate also contains preserved user-provided and upstream-origin material whose notices explicitly require a source/repository-level provenance and license review before broad public redistribution.

Before an open-source, downloadable marketplace, OEM redistribution, or customer source-code redistribution:

- [ ] identify every upstream-origin file that will actually be redistributed;
- [ ] resolve the governing license/restriction for each source;
- [ ] preserve required copyright/attribution/license notices;
- [ ] remove or clean-room replace any source whose redistribution rights are not established;
- [ ] obtain counsel/owner approval for the intended distribution model;
- [ ] freeze a distribution-specific SBOM/provenance report.

A private deployment or commercial evaluation should still preserve these notices. This checklist is not legal advice and is intentionally not replaced by an invented blanket license.
