# Deployment Offerings

1. **Pocket deployment** — one bounded OS (for example DealOS or SecurityOS) with its required connectors.
2. **Department federation** — several pockets under EnterpriseOS supervision.
3. **Enterprise federation** — all required pockets, central policy/state/evidence, SSO, enterprise data and connector certification.
4. **OEM/vertical edition** — selected pockets plus first-party vertical wrappers and connectors.

Commercial packaging can separate software subscription, deployment/integration, connector certification, managed operations and vertical customization.
